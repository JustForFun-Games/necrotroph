Necrotroph

Every death feeds something.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Necrotroph is a mobile-first real-time strategy game set inside Varuun, a dying star-leviathan drifting between galaxies. Four lifeforms, too small for Varuun to see, fight across its organs for every scrap of flesh and every corpse. Whoever holds the most of Varuun when it draws its last breath inherits the body, and in the dark, a leviathan's corpse is a seed. Every unit that falls leaves a body behind, and what each lifeform can do with the dead decides who grows strongest. It runs in the browser as a single HTML file, with peer-to-peer online play for up to four players. THE STORY Varuun swallowed three things it should not have: a comet laced with ancient spores, the carcass of an older leviathan, and a derelict starship, the Hesper. Each carried something that woke up inside it. Varuun's own cells rose to fight back. Now four lifeforms war across its organs, while Varuun's hunter cells, the Wardens, attack them all. The full story is in the game under The story on the main menu. THE CORPSE ECONOMY There are two resources: • Nutrient comes from pink nutrient wells in Varuun's tissue and from scavenging the dead. • Essence comes only from corpses. It pays for elite units and every mutation, so the side that feeds best evolves first. A corpse is worth half the cost of the unit that died, multiplied by how well your lifeform digests that kind of body. Bodies rot away after 75 seconds, and Lumen Lice drift in to steal them if nobody claims them first. CAMPAIGN: THE ATLAS Each lifeform has its own story campaign across a map of Varuun's body: nine organs joined by Lumen channels. • Start in your home organ (the Brood in the Baleen Reefs, the Quiet Choir in the Furnace Gut, the Restoration in the Hesper Wreck, the Grafted in the Lumen Sea) and invade neighbouring organs one battle at a time. • Every organ plays differently. The Starlung is built from bone chambers, the Marrow Spires bristle with Warden Nests, the Lumen Sea is cut by blood channels, and the Hesper Wreck is a maze of metal corridors. • Organs you hold carry over as rewards in later battles: extra Nutrient or Essence, extra workers, more supply, a free mutation, or a faster start. • Rivals spread too. Between battles the other lifeforms claim free organs, and their strongholds fight back harder. • Five acts. Infection, then Fever (Wardens surge and units burn hot), then Collapse (tissue rots and corpses decay faster), then the Last Breath, a four-way battle for Varuun's mind. Last comes the hidden Cradle, and an ending unique to each lifeform. DRIFT: THE LUMEN SEA A second kind of battlefield, set on Varuun's bloodstream.
